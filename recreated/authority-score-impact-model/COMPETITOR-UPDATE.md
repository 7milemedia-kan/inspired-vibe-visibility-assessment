# Competitor assessment copy

The scanner now invites visitors to enter a competitor's URL. Headings, input labels, calls to action, result explanations, and page metadata use the competitor perspective. Server-generated assessment narratives refer to the scanned company in the third person.

The revenue model is framed as a hypothetical competitor scenario using estimated business inputs, not verified competitor financial data. The contact form asks for the visitor's own company without prefilling the competitor's name. Removed the unimplemented promise of an emailed summary from the form and confirmation.

Brand styling, scoring rules, weights, and revenue calculations remain unchanged. TypeScript check and production build passed.
